You don't build a blog page: your site has one already. Publish a post and it appears there, and in a few other places, by itself. The pictures here use Sitebuilder's default template; another template may lay things out differently.

## The blog

Your published posts are listed at `/blog`, newest first, ten to a page. Older posts are on `/blog/page/2` and on, with page numbers at the bottom.

<Screenshot src={blog} alt="The blog: the heading Posts, then three posts as cards, each with its featured picture, title, date and excerpt." />

Each card shows the post's featured image, its title, the date it was published, and its excerpt. Drafts and scheduled posts aren't listed until they go live.

## A post's page

A post shows its title, the date and who wrote it, then its featured image, then your blocks.

<Screenshot src={post} alt="The post Meet our new oven on the site: the title, 27/10/2026 · Sam Rivera, a picture of a loaf in an oven, then the text with two headings." />

At the end are its categories and tags, each a link to the page that lists its posts.

<Screenshot src={postTerms} alt="The end of a post: Categories, News, and Tags, The oven, each as a link." />

A thin bar along the top of the window fills up as visitors scroll through a post. You can turn it off in the Reading settings.

## Category and tag pages

Every category and tag has its own page, listing its posts like the blog does: `/blog/category/news` for the News category, and `/blog/tag/the-oven` for a tag called The oven.

## Help visitors find it

The blog isn't added to your menu by itself. To add it, open **Menus**, add an item and choose:

- **A web address**, with `/blog`, for the whole blog;
- **A category or tag**, for one of their pages. It follows the category if you rename it later.

Or make the blog your home page: in Settings → Reading, set **Home page** to **The latest posts**. Your site's address then lists the posts too, with your site's name and tagline as the heading.

To show a few posts inside a page, such as your three newest on the home page, use the [Latest posts block](/blocks/lists-and-tables#latest-posts).

## Reading settings

Under **Settings → Reading**:

- **Posts per page**: how many posts the blog, and each category and tag page, shows at a time, from 1 to 50.
- **Posts address**: where the blog lives. It's `/blog` to start with; `/news` or `/journal` work too.
- **Show reading progress on posts**: the bar along the top of a post.

Changing the posts address moves every post with it, so you're asked first. Each published post gets a redirect from its old address, so links to it keep working. The address can't be one a page already has.

## The feed and search engines

Your site has a feed at `/feed.xml` with your 20 newest posts, each with its excerpt. People who follow sites in a feed reader can subscribe to it, and their reader finds it from any page of your site.

Published posts, and the category and tag pages, are also in your site's sitemap at `/sitemap.xml`, which tells search engines what to look at.
