**Settings → Reading** decides what visitors see first, and where and how your posts are listed.

- **Home page**: what your site's address shows. Choose a published page, or **The latest posts** to make the blog your home page. [Set the home page and menu](/getting-started/home-and-menu#choose-the-home-page) walks through it.
- **Posts per page**: how many posts the blog, and each category and tag page, shows at a time.
- **Posts address**: where the blog lives, such as `/blog`. Changing it moves every post, with redirects from the old addresses.
- **Show reading progress on posts**: a thin bar along the top of a post that fills as it's read.
- **Search on the site**: a search page at `/search`, linked from the header.

[Where posts appear](/posts/the-blog#reading-settings) explains the post settings in more detail, and [Search on your site](/search-and-cookies/search) the last one.

Press **Save reading settings** when you're done.
