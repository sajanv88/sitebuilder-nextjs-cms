**Settings** in the sidebar holds what applies to the whole site. Only admins can open it. Its sections are listed down the side: **Site**, **Reading**, **Dates**, **Media storage**, **AI**, **Analytics** and **Email**. Each has its own **Save** button, which saves that section only.

## Site

The site's name, its tagline, its language and its address. [Name your site](/getting-started/name-your-site#what-each-field-does) goes through each field.

Change the **Site address** only when your site really moves to a new address, such as from a test address to your own domain. Search engines and your feed are given it for every link to your pages.

## Dates

How dates are written, on your site and in the admin, and the time zone they're in.

<Screenshot src={settingsDates} alt="Settings, Dates: four date formats with 31/12/2026 chosen, and the time zone Europe/London." />

- **Date format**: day first (31/12/2026), month first (12/31/2026), year first (2026-12-31), or the month written out (31 December 2026). It also sets which day calendars start the week on.
- **Time zone**: type a city to find yours, such as Paris. Post dates on your site, every date in the admin, and the time you choose when you schedule a page or post are all in this zone.

Pick the time zone where you and your visitors are. If you schedule a post for 9:00, it goes live at 9:00 there, and clocks changing for summer are taken care of.

The month names follow the site's **Language**, set under Site.
