Everyone who can sign in has a **My account** page, whatever their role. Open it from your name at the bottom of the sidebar, which also has **Sign out**.

## Your name

Your name is shown as the author of what you write, in the admin and under your posts on the site. Change it and press **Save name**. Your posts show the new name straight away.

Your email is what you sign in with, and isn't changed here.

## Your password

Type your **Current password**, then a **New password** of at least 12 characters, and press **Change password**.

You stay signed in where you are, and you're signed out everywhere else, such as on another computer or your phone. That makes it the thing to do if you think someone else knows your password.

A long password made of a few unrelated words is easier to remember and harder to guess than a short one full of symbols. A password manager can make and keep one for you.

If you've forgotten it, see [Forgotten passwords](/users/users#forgotten-passwords).
