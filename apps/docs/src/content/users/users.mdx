**Users** in the sidebar lists everyone who can sign in to your admin, with their role, whether they're active, and how many pages and posts they've written. Only admins see it.

<Screenshot src={users} alt="The Users screen: Alex Chen, an editor; Chris Moore, an author who is banned; Jo Patel, an author with 3 entries; and Sam Rivera, you, the admin, with 15." />

Each person has one role, which decides what they can do. [Roles](/users/roles) explains the three.

## Add someone

1. Press **Add user**.
2. Type their **Name** and **Email**. They sign in with that email.
3. Choose their **Role**.
4. Give them a way in:
   - If your site can send email, tick **Email them a link to choose their password**. The link works once, for a day.
   - Otherwise type a **Password** of at least 12 characters, and give it to them yourself, in person or by a different route than the email address.
5. Press **Add user**.

<Screenshot src={addUser} alt="Add a user: Name Robin Ellis, Email robin@example.com, Role Author, and a Password field." />

They sign in at your site's address followed by `/admin`. Sending email needs [Settings → Email](/settings/email).

## Change someone's account

The **⋯** button at the end of each row has:

- **Change role**: it applies from their next click.
- **Set a new password**: their old one stops working. Tell them the new one yourself.
- **Email a password link**, when your site can send email: they choose a new password themselves.
- **Ban**: they're signed out at once and can't sign in until you choose **Unban**. Everything they wrote stays.
- **Delete**: see below.

You can't ban or delete yourself here, and the site always keeps at least one admin who isn't banned. To hand over, make someone else an admin first.

## Delete someone

Deleting a user can't be undone. If they wrote pages or posts, or uploaded files, choose who gets them: everything moves to that person, history included, and stays on your site.

<Screenshot src={deleteUser} alt="Delete Jo Patel?: their 3 entries, revisions and uploads go to the person you choose, with Give their content to set to Sam Rivera." />

If you only want to stop someone signing in, perhaps for a while, ban them instead.

## Forgotten passwords

On the sign-in page, **Forgot your password?** emails a link to choose a new one, if your site can send email. Otherwise an admin sets a new one for them from this screen.

After 10 wrong passwords, an account is locked, so nobody can guess their way in. It opens again 15 minutes after the last wrong try.
