Everyone who signs in has one of three roles. Give each person the smallest one that lets them do their job: it's easy to change later.

## Admin

Admins can do everything, including the things that change the whole site or who can use it:

- add, change, ban and delete [users](/users/users);
- change the [Settings](/settings/site), including email, media storage, analytics and the AI assistant;
- choose the [template](/appearance/templates);
- create pages with AI.

There must always be at least one admin. Keep it to the people who look after the site.

## Editor

Editors look after all the content, whoever wrote it:

- write, edit, publish, schedule and delete any page or post;
- manage categories and tags;
- see, change and delete any file in [Media](/media/the-library);
- change the [menus](/menus/menus), [redirects](/menus/redirects) and [Appearance](/appearance/appearance);
- use the AI assistant on any page or post.

They can't see Users or Settings, or change the template.

## Author

Authors write posts, and only posts:

- write posts, and publish, schedule, change and trash their own;
- choose categories and tags that already exist;
- upload files, and see, change and delete their own;
- use the AI assistant on their own posts.

They don't see pages at all, can't change other people's posts or files, and can't delete a post for good: an editor or admin deletes it from the trash. In the sidebar, they only see what they can use.

## Everyone

Whatever their role, anyone can change their own name and password in [My account](/users/your-account).

Buttons someone can't use aren't shown to them, and Sitebuilder checks every change again on the server, so a role can't be got round by typing an address.
