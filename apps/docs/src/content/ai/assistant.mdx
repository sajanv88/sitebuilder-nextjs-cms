Once [the assistant is set up](/ai/set-up), the editor has an **Assistant** tab beside **Block** and the page's own settings. It helps with the page you're on. Every answer is a suggestion: nothing changes until you accept it.

## Rewrite text

1. Under **What to rewrite**, choose **The whole page**. Or select a block in the page first, then choose that block: the button is named after it, such as **This text**.
2. In **What to change**, say what you want, such as "Make it warmer, and mention we deliver on Saturdays". The buttons below fill in common ones: **Make it shorter**, **Make it friendlier**, **Fix spelling and grammar** and **Simpler words**.
3. Press **Rewrite**.

<Screenshot src={panel} alt="The Assistant tab: What to rewrite with This text chosen, What to change saying Make it friendlier, the four quick buttons, a Rewrite button, and Search engines with a Suggest button." />

The rewrite shows in the page itself, with a note at the top saying it isn't part of the page yet. Read it there, then press **Accept** to keep it, or **Discard** to go back to what you had. You can't ask for another rewrite until you've done one or the other.

<Screenshot src={rewrite} alt="The editor for Our bread showing the rewritten opening text, with a note above it: The page shows the assistant's rewrite of the text. It isn't part of the page until you accept it. Discard and Accept buttons." />

Accepted changes can be undone like any other, with <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + <Kbd>Z</Kbd>.

## Suggest search text

Under **Search engines**, **Suggest** writes a title and description for search results, and an excerpt for lists, from what's on the page. Press **Use these** to put them in the page's settings, where you can still change them, or **Dismiss**.

<Screenshot src={seo} alt="Search engines with a suggested title, description and excerpt for Our bread, and Dismiss and Use these buttons." />

## Describe a picture

If the model can read pictures (**This model accepts images** in Settings → AI), every alt text field in a block's settings has **Suggest alt text**. The assistant looks at the picture and writes a description. Press **Use it**, or **Dismiss**.

<Screenshot src={alt} alt="An Image block's settings for sourdough.jpg, with a suggested alt text under Suggest alt text describing a round golden-brown sourdough loaf, and Dismiss and Use it buttons." />

If the picture is only decoration, it says so, and suggests leaving the alt text empty so screen readers skip it.

## Check what it wrote

The assistant writes well, but it doesn't know your business. Read everything before you publish: prices, dates, opening hours and names in particular. Where it didn't know something, it writes `[TODO: …]`, and publishing a page with one left asks you first.

Authors see the Assistant tab on their own posts; editors and admins on every page and post.
