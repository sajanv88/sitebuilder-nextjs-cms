**Create with AI** drafts the pages of a whole site from a description: you describe it, answer a few questions, check the plan, and the assistant writes each page as a draft. It's the quickest way from an empty site to something to edit. Only admins can use it, and [the assistant must be set up](/ai/set-up) first.

Open **Pages**, then press **Create with AI**. The steps along the top show where you are: **Describe**, **Questions**, **Plan** and **Pages**.

## 1. Describe the site

In a few sentences, say what the business or project is and what the site is for. For example:

> A website for our family bakery in Ghent. People should find us and order birthday cakes.

Up to 3,000 characters. Only this description and your answers are sent to the provider, nothing else from your site.

## 2. Answer a few questions

The assistant asks up to six questions to fill the gaps, such as who your customers are, or what the site should help them do. Pick from the answers it offers, or write your own. Skip any you'd rather not answer.

## 3. Check the plan

The plan lists the pages it would write, up to 12, the first being the home page, with a title for the site. Leave out the pages you don't want, and change their titles. Then press **Write the pages**: you're told roughly how many tokens it will use before anything starts.

## 4. The pages are written

The pages are written three at a time, each under its parent, and appear in a list as they're done. You can open each one in the editor. Leave the screen open while it works; **Stop** halts it. A page that couldn't be written is marked **Not written**, with **Try again** beside it once the rest are done.

Every page is a **draft**: nothing is published, and visitors see nothing yet.

## Finish

Last, choose what the plan may change on your site:

- **Use the planned menus**: replaces the primary and footer menus with links to the new pages. Each link appears once its page is published.
- **Make the first page the home page**, once it's published.
- **Use the site title and tagline** from the plan.

Then go to **Pages**, read each draft, fill in what it couldn't know, and publish.

<Warning title="Look for [TODO]">
  Where the assistant didn't know something, such as your phone number or prices, it writes `[TODO: …]` instead of making it up. Publishing a page with one left asks you first. Search the page for "TODO" before you publish.
</Warning>

## Changed your mind?

**Discard this design** moves the pages it wrote to the trash, and puts back the menus, home page and title it changed. Pages you've edited since are kept.

You can design up to three sites a day this way.
