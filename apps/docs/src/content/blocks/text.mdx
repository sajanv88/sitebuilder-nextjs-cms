Most pages are mostly words. These five blocks are under **Text** in the **Add** list.

## Heading

A title for a part of the page. Headings break a long page into pieces people can scan, and search engines and screen readers use them to understand how the page is put together.

<Screenshot src={headingText} alt="A heading, How we started, above a Text block with a bold year, a list of three breads and a link." />

- **Text**: the heading itself.
- **Level**: H1 to H6. The page's title is already its H1, so start your headings at **H2**, and use **H3** for the parts inside an H2. Pick by structure, not by size: the template sets how big each level looks.
- **Alignment**: see [How block settings work](/blocks/settings#alignment).

<Note>
  When a page starts with a [Hero](/blocks/marketing#hero), the hero's headline becomes the page's H1 and the page title isn't shown above it.
</Note>

## Text

Paragraphs. Write in the box on the right, then select words to make them bold or italic, turn them into a link, or start a list. The picture above shows a Text block under the heading.

Press <Kbd>Enter</Kbd> for a new paragraph. Paste from another document and the formatting comes with it, cleaned of anything the site can't show.

## Quote

Someone's words, set apart from the text around them, with who said them underneath.

<Screenshot src={quote} alt="A quote with a blue line beside it: The best bread in town, and the queue on Saturday proves it. The Riverside Gazette." />

- **Quote**: the words, up to 1,000 characters.
- **Who said it**: optional. A name, a publication or both.

## Button

A link that looks like a button, for the one thing you most want people to do next.

<Screenshot src={button} alt="A blue button saying See our bread." />

- **Words on the button**: short and about what happens, such as "See our bread" rather than "Click here".
- **Goes to**: a page on your site or a web address. See [Links](/blocks/settings#links).
- **Style**: **Primary** is filled with your brand color, **Secondary** is quieter, and **Ghost** is only the words. Keep Primary for the main action, one per screen if you can.
- **Size**: Small, Medium or Large.
- **Open in a new tab**: leave it off unless the link leaves your site and readers will want to come back.

## Callout

A boxed note that stands out from the text: opening hours, a warning about allergens, a closure.

<Screenshot src={callouts} alt="Four callouts: Information about opening hours in blue, Christmas orders are open in green, a warning about allergens in orange, and a closure in red." />

- **Kind**: **Information**, **Success**, **Warning** or **Danger**. Each has its own color and icon, so the meaning doesn't rest on color alone.
- **Title**: optional, in bold above the text.
- **Text**: the note itself.

Use them sparingly. When everything stands out, nothing does.
