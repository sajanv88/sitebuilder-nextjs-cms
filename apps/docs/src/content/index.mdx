Sitebuilder is a website builder you run yourself. You build pages and posts from blocks in the admin, and visitors see them on your site the moment you publish. There's nothing to rebuild and nothing to deploy.

These pages walk through it one feature at a time, with a picture of each screen.

## Start here

If Sitebuilder is new to you, follow Getting started in order. It takes about fifteen minutes, and at the end you have a named site with a home page and a menu.

1. [Start Sitebuilder](/getting-started/install) on your computer or a server.
2. [Create your account](/getting-started/create-your-account), the first admin.
3. [Name your site](/getting-started/name-your-site) in Settings.
4. [Make your first page](/getting-started/first-page) from blocks, and publish it.
5. [Set the home page and menu](/getting-started/home-and-menu), so visitors find it.

## Two halves: the admin and the site

Everything you do happens in the admin, at your site's address followed by `/admin`. Visitors never see it. They see the public site, which is drawn from what you've published.

You can open the public site from any admin screen with **View site**, in the top right corner.

## Who can do what

Each account has one of three roles.

- **Admins** can do everything. Only they can change Settings, manage Users and switch Templates.
- **Editors** write and publish pages and posts, anyone's. They also look after categories and tags, media, menus, redirects and the Appearance.
- **Authors** write and publish their own posts, and upload pictures for them. They can't open pages or anyone else's posts.

When a page here describes something only an admin can do, it says so. [Roles](/users/roles) has the full list.
