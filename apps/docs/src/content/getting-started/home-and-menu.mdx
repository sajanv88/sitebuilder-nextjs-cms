Your page is live, but a visitor who types your site's address won't land on it, and nothing links to it. Two settings fix that: the home page, and the menu across the top.

## Choose the home page

Until you choose one, the site's address shows your latest posts, like a blog. For most sites a page works better.

1. Open **Settings** and choose **Reading**.
2. Open **Home page** and pick your page. Only published pages are listed. Start typing to search if there are many.
3. Press **Save reading settings**.

<Screenshot src={readingHome} alt="Settings, Reading section, with the Home page list open, showing The latest posts and About us (/about-us)." />

Visitors to your site's address now see that page. Its own address, such as `/about-us`, still works too.

## Build the menu

The primary menu is the row of links across the top of every page. It stays hidden until it has at least one item.

1. Open **Menus** in the sidebar. **Primary menu** is open.
2. Press **Add item**.
3. Type the words for the link in **Label**. They don't have to match the page's title: "About" is fine for a page called "About us".
4. Leave **Goes to** on **A page or post on this site**, and choose the page under **Page or post**.
5. Press **Add item** under the form.

<Screenshot src={menuAddItem} alt="The Add a menu item form: Label About, Goes to A page or post on this site, Page or post About us (/about-us)." />

6. Press **Save primary menu**. Until you do, the menu on your site doesn't change.

<Screenshot src={menuSaved} alt="The primary menu with one item, About, selected, its details beside it, and a Primary menu saved message." />

A menu item that points to a page follows it: if the page's address changes later, the link changes with it.

To change the order, drag an item up or down, or select it and press <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> with an arrow key. **Add under it** puts an item under another one, and the site shows them in a panel that opens from the top item. The **Footer menu** tab works the same way, for links at the bottom of every page.

## Check your site

Open your site's address. The home page is your page, and the menu has your link.
