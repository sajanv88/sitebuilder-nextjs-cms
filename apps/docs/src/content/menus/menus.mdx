Your site has two menus: the **primary menu** across the top of every page, and the **footer menu** at the bottom. You build both under **Menus** in the sidebar. Editors and admins can change them.

[Set the home page and menu](/getting-started/home-and-menu) adds a first link. This page covers the rest.

## The Menus screen

The two tabs hold one menu each. On the left is the menu as a list, with the items under another item indented beneath it. Click an item to see and change it on the right.

<Screenshot src={menusPrimary} alt="The Menus screen, Primary menu tab: Our bread, Blog, About and Visit us, with two items under each of the first three. Blog is selected, and its details are on the right: Label, Goes to A web address, Address /blog." />

Nothing reaches your site until you press **Save primary menu** (or **Save footer menu**). The words beside it say when there are unsaved changes. Save before you leave the screen: changes you haven't saved are lost.

## Add an item

1. Press **Add item**. To put it under another item, select that item and press **Add under it**.
2. Choose where it **Goes to**:
   - **A page or post on this site**: search for it by title.
   - **A category or tag**: its page, listing its posts.
   - **A web address**: a path on your site such as `/blog`, or another site's address starting with `https://`.
   - **Nothing: a group of the items under it**, for a heading with links beneath it. Only top-level items can be groups.
3. Type the **Label**, the words in the menu. Choosing a page or category fills it in with its name; you can shorten it. "About" is fine for a page called "About us".
4. Tick **Open in a new tab** if you want the link to open in a new tab. Keep it for other sites; links within your own site work best in the same tab.
5. Press **Add item**, then save the menu.

<Screenshot src={menuAdd} alt="Add a menu item: Label Gift cards, Goes to A web address, Address https://example.com/gift-cards, Open in a new tab not ticked, and Add item and Cancel buttons." />

A link to a page, post, category or tag follows it: if its address changes later, the menu changes with it. A web address you type stays as you typed it.

## Change, move or remove items

- **Change** an item by selecting it, editing its details, and pressing **Apply changes**. **Undo changes** puts back what was there.
- **Move** an item by dragging its row up or down, or under another item. With the keyboard, select it and press <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> with an arrow key.
- **Remove** an item with **Remove** in its details. Removing an item removes the items under it too, and a group goes when its last item does.

Then save the menu.

Menus have two levels: items, and items under them. The primary menu holds up to 20 items, with up to 12 under each.

## Items the site leaves out

A link to a page or post that isn't published, or to something that's been deleted, is left out of the menu on your site. The Menus screen marks it with a warning sign, and says why when you select it.

<Screenshot src={menuWarning} alt="The details of the Christmas orders item, with a warning: It links to something that isn't published, so the site leaves it out until it is." />

That's handy for a scheduled page: put it in the menu now, and the link appears by itself when the page goes live.

## How the primary menu looks

An item with items under it opens a panel when it's clicked. The panel lists the item itself first, then the ones under it.

On a phone, the menu folds into a menu button. Each item with items under it becomes a heading, with its links below.

## The footer menu

The footer menu works the same way. Each item with items under it becomes a list under its label, which makes groups the natural choice here.
