A template is the design your site is drawn with: the header and menu, how pages and posts are laid out, how the blog lists posts, and the footer. It can also bring starter pages and posts to fill in, menus, and a theme to match. **Templates** in the sidebar lists them. Only admins can change the template.

Your pages and posts don't belong to a template. Change it and everything you've written stays, drawn in the new design.

## The templates

Sitebuilder comes with four. Each card shows its home page on a computer and on a phone, what it brings, and **In use** on the one your site has now.

<Screenshot src={templateGallery} alt="Four template cards: Company, in use, Journal, Ledger and Studio, each with pictures of its home page on a computer and a phone, a description, its starter content, and a Use button." />

- **Company**: for a small business or consultancy. A home page with what you do, reviews and questions, plus Services with prices, About and Contact.
- **Journal**: a blog first. The home page opens on the latest posts, with an About page and a newsletter page.
- **Ledger**: for an accountant or advisory firm. A sober, ruled look, client notes, and Services with fees, About and Contact.
- **Studio**: a portfolio for a design or creative studio. A bold home page, Work, Services with packages, About and Contact.

Whoever runs your site can add more templates. Those are marked **Not reviewed**, as they didn't come with Sitebuilder.

## Change the template

1. Press **Use** on the template's card.
2. Choose what it brings. Sitebuilder checks first, and lists exactly what would be added.
3. Press **Use** in the dialog.

<Screenshot src={templateUse} alt="The Use Journal? dialog: Its colours and type and Its starter pages ticked, Its menus, Its home page and Publish them now not ticked, and a list of the five pages and posts it would add." />

- **Its colours and type** replaces your settings in [Appearance](/appearance/appearance) with the template's. Leave it unticked to keep yours.
- **Its starter pages** adds its pages and posts, with placeholder words such as "[Your company]" to replace with your own.
- **Its menus** replaces your primary and footer menus with links to its pages.
- **Its home page** makes its home page the one visitors see first, once that page is published.
- **Publish them now** publishes the starter pages straight away. Leave it off and they're drafts, so you can fill them in before anyone sees them.

Whatever you tick, the pages and posts you've written aren't changed or removed. If one of yours already has a starter page's address, such as `/about`, the starter page gets a number added to its address instead.

**Apply again** on the template in use offers any of its starter pages you haven't added yet. One that was added before is listed as **Already added** and left alone. One you've moved to the trash is listed as **In the trash** and isn't made again; restore it from the trash if you want it back.

<Tip title="Try it on a draft">
  Leave **Publish them now** off the first time. Open the starter pages in **Pages**, fill them in, and publish each when it's ready.
</Tip>

### Blocks that come with a template

Some templates bring blocks of their own, which appear in the editor's **Add** list. If you change to another template, pages keep those blocks but don't show them on the site. Change back and they reappear.

## The template's options

A template can have options of its own. Those of the template in use are at the top of the Templates screen.

<Screenshot src={templateOptions} alt="Company's options: The header stays at the top of the window, the menu sits beside the site's name, Show the menu's last link as a button not ticked, and Fonts set to The theme's." />

The Company template's are:

- **The header**: stays at the top of the window, hides while scrolling down and comes back on the way up, or scrolls away with the page.
- **Where the menu sits**: beside the site's name, in the middle, or at the far end.
- **Show the menu's last link as a button**: for the one place you most want visitors to go, such as Contact. It stays in the header on a phone too.
- **Fonts**: a pair for headings and text, or the fonts set in Appearance.

Press **Save options**. The change shows on every page of your site, and in the editor, at once.
