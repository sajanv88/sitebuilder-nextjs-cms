Every page and post is written in the same editor. It shows the page as your visitors will see it, with your site's header, colors and fonts, and keeps everything you can change within reach.

<Screenshot src={editorColumns} alt="The editor with a page called Our bread: the Add and Outline tabs on the left, the page in the middle with two columns selected, and the Columns settings on the right." />

## The three parts

**On the left** are the blocks you can add, under **Add**, and the blocks already on the page, under **Outline**.

**In the middle** is the page itself, the canvas. Click any block on it to select it.

**On the right**, **Block** holds the settings of the selected block, and **Page** the settings of the whole page.

Along the top are the title, a badge with the page's status, and the buttons for saving and publishing. [Drafts, scheduling and publishing](/pages/publishing) covers those.

## Add blocks

The **Add** list groups the blocks by kind: text, media, layout and more. Press one to add it after the selected block. If the selected block holds other blocks, like a column or a section, the new one goes inside it instead.

Type in **Search blocks** to find one by name. The pin next to a block puts it at the top of the list, for the ones you use most.

### Quick add

Press <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + <Kbd>K</Kbd> (<Kbd>⌘</Kbd> + <Kbd>K</Kbd> on a Mac) anywhere in the editor. Type part of a block's name, then press <Kbd>Enter</Kbd> to add the one highlighted.

<Screenshot src={quickAdd} alt="Quick add, with but typed into it, listing Button, File download, Hero and Pricing." />

With a block selected, Quick add also offers **Move up**, **Move down**, **Duplicate** and **Remove** for it.

## See the page at other sizes

The buttons above the canvas change how wide it is.

- **Fit** fills the space you have, which is the most comfortable for writing.
- **Desktop**, **Tablet** and **Phone** show the page at those widths.

<Screenshot src={canvasPhone} alt="The canvas at Phone width: the menu is a button, and the two columns sit one under the other." />

On a phone, columns sit one under another and the menu folds into a button, exactly as visitors on phones will see it.

## The outline

**Outline** lists every block on the page, with the blocks inside columns and sections set in under them. It's the quickest way to find a block on a long page, and to select one that's hard to click on the canvas, such as a column.

<Screenshot src={outline} alt="The Outline tab: a Text block, then Columns with two Columns inside, each holding a Heading and a Text." />

## The Page tab

**Page** holds what belongs to the whole page rather than one block.

<Screenshot src={pageTab} alt="The Page tab: Reading with a word count, Outline, Publishing with date and time, and Address with Slug and Parent page." />

- **Reading** counts the words and the minutes they take to read. **Outline** lists the page's headings, so you can check they make sense in order.
- **Publishing** sets a date and time to publish later.
- **Address** sets the end of the page's address, its slug, and which page it sits under.
- **Featured image** is the picture shown with the page in lists, and when someone shares it.
- **Background** gives this page a colour of its own, or the whole site one.
- **Summary** is a short excerpt, for lists of pages and search results.
- **Search engines** sets the title and description search results show, if you want them different from the page's own.

## Keyboard shortcuts

The keyboard button in the top bar lists them too.

- <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + <Kbd>Z</Kbd> undoes the last change.
- <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> + <Kbd>Z</Kbd>, or <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + <Kbd>Y</Kbd>, redoes it.
- <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + <Kbd>S</Kbd> saves.
- <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + <Kbd>K</Kbd> opens Quick add.
- <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> + <Kbd>↑</Kbd> and <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> + <Kbd>↓</Kbd> move the selected block up and down.
- <Kbd>Delete</Kbd> removes the selected block.

On a Mac, use <Kbd>⌘</Kbd> where it says <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>. While you're typing in a text box, undo, <Kbd>Delete</Kbd> and the arrows work on your text instead.
