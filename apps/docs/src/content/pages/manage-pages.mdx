**Pages** in the sidebar lists every page on your site: what it's called, its address, who wrote it, and whether visitors can see it. Editors and admins can open it. Authors write posts only, so they don't see it.

<Screenshot src={pagesList} alt="The Pages list with five pages: one scheduled, one draft and three published, one of them at /about-us/our-team." />

## What the status means

Every page is in one of three states, shown as a badge.

- **Published** pages are on your site now.
- **Draft** pages are only in the admin. Nobody outside it can see them.
- **Scheduled** pages go live by themselves at the date and time in the Date column.

The tabs above the list show each group on its own, with a count: **All**, **Published**, **Drafts**, **Scheduled** and **Trash**. The **All** tab leaves out the trash.

## Find a page

Type in **Search** to narrow the list by title. Click a column heading to sort by it, and click it again to reverse the order. The list starts with the most recent date first.

**Columns** chooses which columns show. **Rows per page**, under the list, shows more at once on a big site.

Click a page's title to open it in the editor. The **⋯** button at the end of each row has the same **Edit**, and **Move to trash**.

## Pages inside pages

A page can sit under another one. In the example, Our team is under About us, so its address is `/about-us/our-team`.

To put a page under another, open it and choose **Parent page** in the **Page** tab, under **Address**. The list shows each page's full address under its title.

## Move pages to the trash

To get rid of a page, choose **Move to trash** from its **⋯** menu, or from the editor's top bar. For several at once, tick their rows: a bar appears above the list with **Move to trash**.

<Screenshot src={pagesSelected} alt="The Pages list with one row ticked and a bar above it saying 1 selected, with Move to trash and Clear selection." />

A page in the trash is off your site straight away, but nothing is lost yet. Open the **Trash** tab to see it. From its **⋯** menu:

- **Restore** brings it back as it was, published or not. If another page has taken its address meanwhile, it comes back as a draft with a new one.
- **Delete permanently** removes it for good. Sitebuilder asks first, because this can't be undone.

<Screenshot src={trash} alt="The Trash tab with Christmas orders in it, and its menu open showing Restore and Delete permanently." />

<Tip>
  Changed a page's address instead of deleting it? You don't need to do anything for old links: Sitebuilder sends visitors from the old address to the new one.
</Tip>
